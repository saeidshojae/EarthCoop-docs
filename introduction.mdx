EarthCoop is a cooperative platform built around one idea: people with shared goals should have a trusted, structured space to organize, invest together, and take collective action — whether they live on the same street or on opposite sides of the world. By combining intelligent group formation, real-time communication, community-driven project investment, and an AI-powered assistant into a single platform, EarthCoop gives you everything you need to move from intention to impact.

## What Problem Does EarthCoop Solve?

Collaboration at scale is hard. Professionals in the same field rarely find each other across city or country borders. Communities that want to fund local projects have no shared financial infrastructure. Groups that form online quickly fragment without the right tools to make decisions, manage money, or stay aligned. EarthCoop addresses all of these pain points in one place — giving you the structure of a cooperative with the reach of a global network.

## Key Features

- **Intelligent Group Formation** — Groups are organized by profession, specialty, and geographic scope (from neighborhood to global), so you always find the most relevant communities for your goals and expertise.
- **Real-Time Group Chat with Polls and Elections** — Every group has a live communication channel with built-in polling and election tools, enabling transparent, democratic decision-making at any scale.
- **Project Submission and Community Investment** — Any member can submit a project proposal to their group. Fellow members review, discuss, and invest directly, turning collective capital into real-world action.
- **NajmBahar Integrated Financial System** — EarthCoop's native financial layer, NajmBahar, handles all group-level transactions, investment accounting, and fund distribution with full transparency.
- **NajmHoda AI Assistant** — NajmHoda is an AI assistant embedded throughout the platform. It answers questions, helps you find the right groups or projects, and guides you through complex workflows.
- **Multilingual Support (English and Persian/Jalali)** — The platform is fully available in both English and Persian, including Jalali (Solar Hijri) calendar support for date-sensitive features, so that no language barrier prevents participation.
- **Support Ticket System** — A built-in support system lets you raise issues, track resolutions, and communicate directly with the EarthCoop team — all without leaving the platform.

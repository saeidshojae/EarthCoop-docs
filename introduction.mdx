EarthCoop is a cooperative socio-economic platform designed to connect participation from the local community up through wider geographic and professional structures. Its current product combines structured registration, canonical location/governance, automatically assigned system groups, communication and participation tools, project workflows, financial-domain services, support, and an evolving AI assistant.

The documentation uses three capability labels so an architectural goal is never mistaken for a live feature.

## Current product

The current product includes a multi-step member-registration flow, profile and Primary Residence data, canonical Location/Governance, system-group membership derived from member information, group communication and participation surfaces, systemic-election services, project workflows, Najm Bahar domain services, support tickets, and a member-facing Najm Hoda interaction surface.

EarthCoop's system groups are not simply generic public or private communities. Membership is formed from structured dimensions such as public assemblies, professional/specialty identity, and age/gender groupings across relevant geographic levels. User-created groups are a separate concept and are documented separately from those automatic system memberships.

## In development

Some domains are implemented but are still being separated behind clearer, stable client contracts or are receiving further product hardening. Examples include the versioned API/mobile boundary, parts of the wider Najm Bahar client surface, and broader Najm Hoda integrations. An endpoint or internal service existing in the repository does not by itself mean that the capability is a stable public API.

## Planned

EarthCoop's broader architecture includes additional marketplace, mobile, economic, and AI-assisted capabilities. Planned architecture is documented as planned until there is implementation evidence that supports a stronger status. Foundational or reference documents may define normative rules before every related product surface is available.

## Language status

Persian is the canonical language for EarthCoop's current foundational/reference document set. English product guides are being audited and corrected against the current product. Arabic is part of the multilingual documentation architecture, but Arabic renditions are published as current only after they are reviewed against a current source. Do not interpret documentation-translation status as a statement about legal effect.

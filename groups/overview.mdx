Groups are a core operating structure in EarthCoop, but not every group is created or joined in the same way. The most important distinction is between **system groups**, which EarthCoop materializes from verified member dimensions, and **user-created groups**, which members may establish for separate organizational purposes.

## System groups

When registration and the required profile dimensions are complete, EarthCoop automatically resolves the member's relevant system memberships. A member does not need to search for or manually create these groups.

The system model contains three broad families:

1. **Public assemblies** — geographic public-governance groups tied to the member's Primary Residence and the applicable governance hierarchy.
2. **Professional and specialty groups** — groups derived from occupation, profession and expertise across the applicable geographic hierarchy.
3. **Age/gender groups** — system groups derived from those member dimensions across the applicable geographic hierarchy.

A single person can therefore appear in several system groups at the same geographic level because each group represents a different dimension of participation.

## User-created groups

**User-created groups** are separate from the automatic system structure. They can support member initiatives such as organizations, unions, companies or other cooperative activities where the relevant product flow permits them.

Creating a user-created group does not replace a public assembly, professional/specialty group, or age/gender system group. Likewise, settings on a user-created group must not be read as changing the constitutional or governance status of system groups.

## Geographic and governance hierarchy

System memberships connect local participation to wider levels. The canonical Location/Governance model resolves the member's **base governance** area from Primary Residence and then connects that membership upward through applicable wider governance areas.

The topology is not a fixed neighborhood → city-only chain. EarthCoop supports cases such as a city without an urban region, an urban region without a neighborhood, and a village without a neighborhood. See [Location & Governance](/governance/location-and-governance) for the residence and topology rules.

## Active participation and observation

System membership does not mean identical permissions at every level.

- At the member's current local/base governance level, the relevant **Active** membership can carry direct participation rights according to the group/domain rules.
- At applicable higher system levels, the member can be represented as an **Observer** rather than receiving an automatic right to intervene directly in every discussion, poll, or systemic election.
- **Temporary Active** is a limited participation role used where the current rules permit additional participation in small groups. It is not equivalent to Active for systemic-election eligibility: it does **not** create systemic-election voting/selectability rights and does not count as an ordinary Active member for that purpose.
- Elected **Inspector** and **Manager** roles remain member roles with the systemic voting/selectability rights preserved by the current eligibility contract; election to responsibility does not erase the member's personal voting right.

Do not infer a role change merely because a community grows or because an election occurs. Role and election eligibility are governed by the canonical membership/election rules.

## Pending location memberships

A member can complete the registration/residence flow with a valid proposed base location that is still pending review. Related system memberships can therefore exist in a pending state while the proposal awaits confirmation.

Community support for a pending location is evidence for review priority/readiness. **Support is not approval.** Reaching the configured support threshold does not itself make the proposed location administratively approved.

## What can happen inside groups

EarthCoop group surfaces can include communication, posts, polls, systemic-election items, and project/community activity, but availability and permission are domain- and role-dependent. A guide should not imply that every member can perform every action in every group.

- **Chat and messaging** follow group/session participation rules.
- **Polls** are a decision/opinion domain distinct from formal system elections.
- **System elections** select official responsibilities such as Manager and Inspector under the systemic-election lifecycle.
- **Projects** and economic activity follow their own project and Najm Bahar contracts.
- **Najm Hoda** may assist through implemented interaction/orchestration surfaces, but it does not become the group's legislator or replace member decisions.

# EarthCoop Foundational Documents

EarthCoop's foundational corpus defines the project's principles, rights, governance, economic system, digital architecture, judicial framework, local communities, ethical commitments, and technical standards.

<Note>
The Persian text is the authoritative source language. English translations are provided for accessibility and international review. Until a translation is formally approved, the corresponding Persian version prevails in case of conflict.
</Note>

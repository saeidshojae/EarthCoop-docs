# Package B — LOC / DG / JUD alignment plan

This plan continues from Package A without reopening already-settled FC/CH/CO architecture.

## LOC
- Replace neighborhood-as-universal-base with `base governance area`.
- Separate location hierarchy from governance hierarchy.
- Move participation-state ownership to LOC/EX boundaries; DG only implements digital state.
- Rework temporary-active rules so they do not silently grant voting/election-threshold rights.
- Make upward representation originate from the actual base governance level rather than always from neighborhood.
- Narrow local-offense definitions to LOC-specific conduct and cross-reference ECON/DG/JUD for their domains.
- Replace weak external-law wording (`تا حد امکان`) with binding-applicable-law compliance language.
- Clarify local parameter ownership and canonical registry/delegation.
- Fix stale 0.1 version residue in the 0.2 text through the next amendment/consolidation path, not by rewriting history.

## DG
- Recast DG as the digital/data/security subject law, not a supplement subordinate to EX.
- Remove substantive ownership of location and election rules from DG; consume LOC/EX rules digitally.
- Remove hard-coded election counts and keep only parameterized digital enforcement.
- Clarify privacy/feedback visibility as implementation of legally owned visibility rules.
- Keep DG-native digital offenses; move adjudication and final sanctions to JUD while preserving narrowly tailored emergency protective controls.
- Correct amendment language so peer subject laws are not treated as hierarchical superiors/subordinates.
- Fix stale 0.1 version residue through the next amendment/consolidation path.

## JUD
- Recast JUD as the justice/procedure/remedy law with only justice-system-native substantive offenses.
- Add legality/non-retroactivity safeguards and clearer interim-protective-action boundaries.
- Replace duplicate membership/election/economic/digital offense catalogues with references to the competent subject laws.
- Keep transitional common-resource/environment provisions only where no dedicated subject law yet exists.
- Strengthen whistleblower good-faith standard.
- Replace optional-sounding external-law compliance wording with binding applicable-law compliance subject to lawful challenge/exemption.
- Remove `قوانین موضوعی بالادست` language; use peer-law impact review.
- Fix stale 0.1 version residue through the next amendment/consolidation path.

## Safety
All Package B outputs are amendment drafts only: unregistered, non-effective, no registry/manifest/public-baseline promotion, no historical-release rewrite.

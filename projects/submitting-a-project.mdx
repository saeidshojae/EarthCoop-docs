Submitting a project on EarthCoop makes your cooperative idea visible to the entire community for potential investment. Before community members can invest in your initiative, a platform admin reviews your proposal to confirm it meets quality and transparency standards. This page walks you through every step, from filling in project details to handling reviewer feedback.

<Note>
  You must have an active NajmBahar account before you can submit a project. If you have not yet created one, go to **NajmBahar → Agreement** and complete the setup first.
</Note>

## How to Submit a Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to My Projects">
    From your NajmBahar dashboard, open **Projects → My Projects**, then click **Create New Project**. If you are submitting on behalf of a cooperative group, navigate to the group's NajmBahar section instead. You must be a group manager (role 2 or 3) to submit on a group's behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill In Project Details">
    Complete all required fields in the project form. See [Required Fields](#required-fields) below for the full list. Your project is saved and enters the review queue as soon as you submit the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Three-Level Category">
    Use the cascading category selectors to choose your occupational field:

    - Select a **Level 1** sector from the top-level list.
    - The **Level 2** selector loads the sub-sectors for your Level 1 choice.
    - The **Level 3** selector loads the specific fields under your Level 2 choice.

    All three levels are required. The platform validates that each level is a proper child of the one above it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Your Business Plan">
    Attach your full business plan as a PDF, DOC, or DOCX file (maximum 10 MB). This document gives reviewers and investors the detail they need to evaluate your project. The file is required on first submission and optional on updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Funding Goal and Terms">
    Enter the **investment amount** (in GOL), the **profit percentage** you are offering investors, and the **investment duration in months**. These figures are locked in once the project is approved, so set them carefully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for Review">
    When everything looks correct, click **Submit**. Your project is saved with a `pending` status and the admin team is notified immediately. You can still edit the project while it is in `pending` status — any changes are saved to the same submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Required Fields

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Clear, descriptive name for the project (max 255 characters). |
| **Category (Levels 1–3)** | All three levels of the occupational field hierarchy are required. |
| **Project Type** | `public` (visible to all users) or `private`. |
| **Summary** | A concise description of the project (max 3,000 characters). |
| **Investment Amount** | The total capital you are seeking, in GOL. Enter as a number with up to two decimal places. |
| **Profit Percentage** | The return you are offering investors, as a percentage (0–100). |
| **Duration (Months)** | How long the investment runs (1–600 months). |
| **Business Plan File** | PDF, DOC, or DOCX, max 10 MB. Required on first submission. |

## Project Statuses

| Status | What It Means for You |
| --- | --- |
| **draft** | Saved but not yet submitted. Only you can see it. |
| **pending** | Submitted and awaiting admin assignment. You can still edit and update your project at this stage. |
| **under\_review** | An admin is actively evaluating your project. No further edits are possible until a decision is made. |
| **approved** | Your project is live and open to investors. |
| **rejected** | Not approved. You can edit your project and resubmit for another review round. |
| **archived** | Removed from the active listing by an admin. |

<Warning>
  Only **approved** projects appear in the investment marketplace. Projects in any other status are invisible to investors.
</Warning>

## The Review Process

After you submit, the admin team takes the following steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Admin Opens the Review">
    An admin picks up your pending project and sets its status to `under_review`. This signals that active evaluation has started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Admin Evaluates the Project">
    The reviewer reads your summary, examines the business plan, checks the category and financial terms, and assesses whether the project meets platform standards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decision: Approve, Reject, or Request Revision">
    The admin takes one of three actions:

    - **Approve** — Status becomes `approved`. Your project goes live immediately.
    - **Reject** — Status becomes `rejected` and a rejection reason is stored. You are notified and can revise and resubmit.
    - **Request Revision** — Status becomes `rejected` and detailed revision notes are attached. Functionally the same as a rejection — you revise and resubmit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every admin action — including who reviewed, what decision was made, and when — is recorded permanently in the project's review history. You can view this log on your project detail page.

## Revision and Resubmission

If your project is rejected, you retain full edit access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the Rejection Reason">
    Open the project detail page. The reviewer's comments and rejection reason are displayed. Check the review history for any additional notes from earlier review rounds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the Project">
    Update the fields or business plan file to address the reviewer's concerns. You can save your changes as many times as needed while the project is in `rejected` status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resubmit">
    Once you are satisfied with your revisions, click **Resubmit for Review**. The status resets to `pending`, the rejection reason is cleared, and the resubmission timestamp is recorded. The project enters the review queue again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Address all points raised in the rejection reason before resubmitting. Incomplete revisions are likely to result in another rejection and delay your project going live.
</Tip>

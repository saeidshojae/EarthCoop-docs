EarthCoop uses a canonical **Location/Governance** model to connect a member's real Primary Residence to the correct local governance area and to the wider system-group hierarchy. This is not a free-text address field and it is not limited to one fixed city structure.

## Primary Residence

During registration, the member selects the geographic path for their **Primary Residence**. The path begins at wider levels and continues until the member reaches the **base governance** level required by the selected settlement topology.

That base is the lowest governance unit that actually exists for the selected place. The registration flow does not invent a missing layer merely to force every member through the same hierarchy.

## Topology-aware base governance

Current EarthCoop behavior explicitly supports different settlement structures, including:

- a city that has urban regions and neighborhoods;
- a **city without an urban region**;
- an **urban region without a neighborhood**;
- a **village without a neighborhood**.

When no lower governance unit exists, the highest valid available level becomes the member's base governance area. A city without regions does not need a fake region; an urban region without neighborhoods does not need a fake neighborhood; and a village without neighborhoods can itself be the relevant base.

## Registration stops at the governance base

The mandatory registration path ends when the valid base-governance level has been selected. The registration step should not force a member to provide Street, Alley, Complex or Building information.

**Street and smaller address levels are optional after registration.** A member can later complete a more precise local address from the profile/location surface where the local topology supports it.

Supported micro-location paths can include combinations such as:

- Street → Building;
- Street → Alley → Building;
- Street → Complex → Building;
- Street → Alley → Complex → Building.

The local-address path provides additional precision; it does not replace the member's canonical Primary Residence or base-governance membership.

## When a place is missing

A member may propose a valid missing location or governance unit through the supported proposal flow. The proposal enters a pending-review state rather than becoming silently approved.

A pending base-level proposal does not automatically have to block registration. EarthCoop can preserve the selected pending residence and the related pending system-group relationships while the proposal is reviewed.

## Community support and review readiness

Other eligible nearby members may support a pending proposal according to the configured support policy. Distinct-user support can move the proposal toward **ready for review** status and make it more visible in the administrative review queue.

<Warning>
  **Support is not approval.** Reaching the configured support threshold does not automatically approve the location, structural claim, or governance area. Approval remains a separate review decision.
</Warning>

If a pending proposal is ultimately rejected or superseded, the product must reconcile the affected residence/governance relationships according to the canonical cleanup/migration rules rather than leaving a silent contradictory state.

## System groups derived from residence

The canonical Primary Residence and governance path are consumed by EarthCoop's system-group architecture. They determine the applicable public-assembly membership and help scope professional/specialty and age/gender system memberships across wider levels.

At the local/base level the member may hold the relevant Active membership. Wider levels can be Observer memberships. This is why changing an address is not merely a cosmetic profile edit: it can change canonical governance and group relationships.

## Governance topology and elections

Official systemic-election eligibility follows the canonical official governance topology. Community/micro-location relationships and Temporary Active participation do not automatically become official systemic-election voting/selectability rights.

Current role semantics preserve systemic voting/selectability for Active members and elected Inspectors/Managers, while Observer, Guest and Temporary Active roles do not gain those rights merely by being present in the group.

## Correcting residence later

If a member selected an incorrect residence or the local topology is later clarified, the profile/Location & Governance flow should be used to establish the corrected canonical relationship. Corrections must preserve auditability and must not be implemented by silently editing unrelated higher-level memberships.

<Info>
  Product UI around proposal-support progress, invitations for nearby supporters, and high-volume administrative review may continue to improve without changing the canonical rule: residence truth, support, review and approval are separate concepts.
</Info>

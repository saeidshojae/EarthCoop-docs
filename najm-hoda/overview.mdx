NajmHoda is EarthCoop's built-in AI assistant — always available to answer your questions, walk you through platform features, and connect you with the right resources. Whether you are new to the cooperative or need a quick reminder of how something works, NajmHoda is the fastest way to get help without leaving the page you are on.

## What NajmHoda can do

NajmHoda handles a broad range of support tasks directly in the chat widget:

- **Answer platform questions** — from account setup and group membership to NajmBahar finances and project investment.
- **Guide you through features** — ask how to create a cooperative group, submit a project, or configure your notification preferences, and NajmHoda responds with step-by-step instructions.
- **Surface knowledge base articles** — every response automatically searches published KB articles, blog posts, and FAQs to back up its answer with authoritative references.
- **Suggest related questions** — NajmHoda proactively surfaces follow-up questions so you can explore a topic further without typing another query.
- **Escalate to human support** — when a question falls outside NajmHoda's knowledge, it flags the conversation for the support team and can help you open a ticket.

## The Steward agent

When you chat with NajmHoda as a regular member, you interact with the **Steward** agent. The Steward is the user-facing AI mode, purpose-built to search the knowledge base and respond in a clear, friendly tone. It searches across three content sources on every question:

| Source | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Knowledge Base | 20\+ published articles across 18 categories |
| Blog posts | Platform news, tips, and feature announcements |
| FAQs | Common questions with verified answers |

The Steward formats every response the same way so you always know where to look for more detail:

- **Answer** — a direct, plain-language response to your question.
- **Steps** — numbered instructions when the answer involves an action.
- **Example** — a concrete illustration when it helps clarify the steps.
- **Related KB articles** — links to the source articles the answer drew from.
- **Related questions** — suggested follow-ups you may want to ask next.

<Note>
  Admin users have access to additional specialist agents. If you are a platform administrator, your chat widget may offer engineering, pilot, or guide modes in addition to the Steward.
</Note>

## How to get started

Open the chat widget from any page on the platform and type your question in plain language — no special syntax required. NajmHoda understands natural questions like "How do I invite someone to my group?" or "Where do I see my NajmBahar balance?".

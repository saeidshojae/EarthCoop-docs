Membership fees are annual contributions that sustain EarthCoop's cooperative operations. When you open a NajmBahar account, your account is created but remains in a limited state until you pay the fee for the current membership year. The fee is not deducted automatically — you choose when to pay it, giving you full control over the timing.

## How the fee is structured

The annual membership fee is split into three portions at the moment of payment and sent to three separate system accounts simultaneously:

| Portion | Destination | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Membership amount | Membership pool account | Cooperative operating fund |
| Insurance amount | Insurance pool account | Member insurance reserve |
| Burn amount | Burn pool account | Cooperative burn pool |

The exact amounts for each portion are set by platform administrators in the NajmBahar settings. The default split is **6 Bahar** to membership, **3 Bahar** to insurance, and **3 Bahar** to the burn pool — a total of **12 Bahar** per year. You can confirm the current totals in the membership fee payment modal before confirming.

<Note>
  Membership fees are paid per **membership anniversary year**, calculated from the date your account was created. The system tracks whether you have paid for the current anniversary period and blocks duplicate payments for the same year.
</Note>

## Check your fee status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your NajmBahar dashboard">
    Navigate to **NajmBahar → Dashboard**. If your annual fee is due, a prompt appears near the top of the page with the total amount and a **Pay Now** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the fee details modal">
    Click **Pay Membership Fee** to open the information modal. The modal shows:

    - Whether you have already paid for the current year
    - The total fee amount and its three-way breakdown
    - Your sub-account's current active balance
    - Your next membership anniversary date
    - A link to create a sub-account if you do not have one yet
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pay your membership fee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fund your sub-account">
    Membership fees are paid from the **active balance** of your first active sub-account. Before paying, make sure that sub-account holds at least the total fee amount in active funds. If you have no sub-account yet, create one first and transfer funds from your main account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the payment modal">
    From the dashboard or wallet, click **Pay Membership Fee**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the breakdown">
    Confirm the three-way split shown in the modal. The amounts are determined by platform settings and are not editable by members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Click **Pay**. The system executes three separate transfers from your sub-account — one for each portion. The payment is protected against accidental double-submission, so confirming twice is safe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm success">
    After payment, you are redirected to the NajmBahar dashboard with a success message showing the total amount paid. Your account's full-active status is restored.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The fee is drawn from **active balance only**. Faded balance cannot be used for membership fee payments. If your sub-account's active balance is below the total fee, top it up from your main account first using the **Transfer To Sub-Account** action.
</Warning>

## Fee payment history

To review past membership fee payments:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your sub-account detail page">
    Navigate to **NajmBahar → Sub-Accounts** and open the sub-account used for fee payments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the transaction list">
    Each year's membership fee payment produces three transaction entries — one per split destination (membership pool, insurance pool, and burn pool). All three entries are recorded with the same payment year so you can easily identify a complete annual payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also see a summary of fee status directly in the dashboard widget, which shows whether your account is fully active for the current year.

## Membership anniversary and due dates

Your membership year runs from your account creation date, not from January 1st. For example, if you joined on 15 March 2024, your next fee is due on 15 March 2025.

The fee info modal always shows you:

- **Membership date** — the original date your account was created
- **Next anniversary** — the next date your annual fee becomes due

<Tip>
  Pay your membership fee a few days before your anniversary date to ensure your account remains continuously active without any interruption to transfers or investment access.
</Tip>

## What happens if you don't pay

Your NajmBahar account continues to exist and you can still receive incoming transfers. However, certain platform features that require a fully active account may be restricted until you pay the current year's fee. You can pay at any time — the system checks the current anniversary year and accepts exactly one payment per year per user.

## Referral bonus and initial balance

When you first open a NajmBahar account, an initial balance is deposited automatically. A portion of this initial balance is designated as **active** and the rest as **faded**, according to the platform's current balance ratio settings. Additionally, if you were referred to the platform by another member, a referral bonus is automatically transferred from your new account to your referrer's account as a one-time system operation.

<Info>
  The referral bonus transfer and the initial funding deposit both appear in the transaction history of your main account. They are system-initiated operations and do not affect your ability to pay the membership fee — only your active sub-account balance matters for fee payment.
</Info>
